Weekly Traditions

Mondays (Monthly)

Toast Masters Meetup

Build confidence, sharpen your communication skills, and grow as a leader in a supportive, welcoming environment. Toast Masters is a public speaking and leadership meetup where you can practice presentations, share ideas, and receive constructive feedback all while enjoying a relaxed, kava-fueled atmosphere. Perfect for anyone looking to improve their voice, presence, and leadership potential.

Schedule: Monthly Meetup

3rd Tuesday

Speed Networking

Ready to grow your network? Join us for our next Speed Networking! Think speed dating, but instead of a date, you'll leave with valuable business connections, fresh ideas, and new opportunities!

Schedule: Every 3rd Tuesday

Wednesdays

Valrico Founders Table (Midday)

Take a break in the middle of your week for good conversations and great connections! Join local professionals, creatives, and entrepreneurs for a relaxed midday meetup at Quigs. Share ideas, build relationships, and collaborate over a cup of kava, kratom, or coffee. Whether you’re growing your business or just looking to meet like-minded people, this is the perfect place to network, recharge, and keep your week moving strong.

Time: 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM

Wednesdays

Movie Night

Join us every Wednesday at 7pm for some cozy flicks, good vibes, and free popcorn with your drink!🍿 Follow our Instagram & Facebook @quigskavalounge to see what weekly movie we are watching!

Time: 7:00 PM

Thursdays

Weekly Men’s Meetup

Join us for our Weekly Men’s Meetup: a relaxed space to connect, share experiences, and build meaningful friendships. Whether you’re looking for good conversation, personal growth, networking, or simply a place to unwind with other guys in the community, everyone is welcome. No pressure, no agenda: just real conversations, support, and connection. Come as you are and be part of a group that values community, encouragement, and growth.

Time: 7:00 PM
